Software

R packages, tools, and apps I build and maintain.

From CRAN packages for genomic data to Shiny apps for exploring chickpea pangenome data — code written to make plant genomics analysis more reproducible.

CRAN packages

Published, peer-reviewed R packages

R package

refseqR

A framework of common computational operations working with RefSeq entries (GenBank). Provides summary information at three levels: gene, transcript, and protein sequences.

R package · dataset

airqualityES

Daily air quality measurements in Spain over 18 years (2001–2018), across several pollutants. Openly published by the Government of Spain, tidied into a single dataframe for researchers, journalists, or the general public.

Annotation packages

Gene family & genome resources

Pipeline

GeneHummus

An automated pipeline with high specificity and sensitivity in extracting proteins from the RefSeq database (NCBI) based on protein domain organization. Useful for plant gene family characterization, phylogenetic construction, or gene expression profiling.

optimalPlan

Identifies the optimal sampling plan for a qPCR experiment within a given budget, allocating replicates (RNA, cDNA, qPCR) where they're expected to have the greatest benefit.
